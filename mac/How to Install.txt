NRG to MP3 — How to Install
===========================

Needs: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 or later) and macOS 13 Ventura or later.

1. Drag "NRG to MP3" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click "NRG to MP3".

3. The first time, macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious software
   (because it isn't distributed through the App Store). To allow it, once:

     • Click "Done" (or "OK") on that message.
     • Open  System Settings → Privacy & Security.
     • Scroll down to the message about "NRG to MP3" and click "Open Anyway".
     • Enter your password if asked, then click "Open Anyway" again.

   After that it opens normally, like any other app.

   (Alternative for Terminal users: run
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/NRG to MP3.app"
    then open the app.)

4. Optional: to keep it in the Dock, right-click its Dock icon while it's open
   and choose Options → Keep in Dock.

Everything the app needs is included — MP3 export works without installing
anything else. Burning and copying CDs need a CD drive (for example an
Apple USB SuperDrive).
